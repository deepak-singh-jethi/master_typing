Typing Master — Phase 4C.3 validation report
Date: 2026-08-01
Release: 0.4.3

Release scope
-------------
Phase 4C.3 implements the Practice and Result Experience on top of Phase 4C.2 and the Phase 4C.1.1 sync hotfix.

Implemented
-----------
- Four primary quick-start presets with additional guided presets behind progressive disclosure.
- Advanced recipe controls behind one expandable panel.
- Fixed 1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 15, and 20-minute choices.
- Custom time targets from 1 to 60 minutes.
- Custom word targets from 10 to 5,000 words.
- Distinct Retry same text and Fresh text, same recipe actions.
- Exact mistake-recovery sessions preserving keys, bigrams, confusion pairs, and error words.
- Same-mode comparison for practice, lessons, diagnostics, and benchmark tests.
- Comparison keys include content type, purpose, goal, planned time/length, category, document style, difficulty, and feature policy.
- One primary diagnosis and one explicit next action per result.
- Exact lesson-mastery blocker explanations.
- Comparable-session metadata retained through compact cloud sync.
- Timed text budget increased to at least 180 WPM plus safety margin.
- Number word-count generation corrected.
- Immediate high-focus sentence repetition corrected.
