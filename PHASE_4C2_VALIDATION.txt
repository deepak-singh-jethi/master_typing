Release scope
-------------
Phase 4C.2 implements the Content and Difficulty System on top of the Phase 4C.1.1 cloud-sync hotfix.

Implemented
-----------
- Four word-frequency tiers.
- QWERTY hand, finger, row, and movement metadata.
- Same-finger transition, hand-alternation, repeated-letter, uncommon-bigram, travel, hand-balance, and weak-finger measures.
- Easy, balanced, challenging, and learner-stage adaptive motor difficulty.
- Progressive punctuation, capitals, numbers, and symbols.
- Natural sentence generation for General, Study, Work, Technology, and Government categories.
- Practical document families for Everyday, Office email, Forms/data, Study, Government, and Technology.
- Email, meeting-note, application-record, weekly-report, notice, and data-entry templates.
- Content fingerprints, recent-history exclusions, freshness retries, and immediate-repeat prevention.
- Difficulty and progressive-feature controls in Practice Studio.
- Motor/repetition quality shown in session results.
- Compact generation metadata retained in local attempts and Supabase session metadata.
- Existing Phase 4C.1 recipes, local data, guest mode, and Supabase schema remain compatible.
